🎙️ KIN GUARDIAN VOICE SETUP

Enable Microphone Access

To stream real-time voice guidance safely, Chrome needs you to enable microphone access for the Kin extension.

1
Click the orange "Allow Microphone Access" button below.
2
When Chrome displays its native popup near the address bar, click "Allow".
💡 No popup appearing? Look at the address bar above: click the tune / sliders icon (🎛️) directly to the left of the URL and switch Microphone to Allow.
